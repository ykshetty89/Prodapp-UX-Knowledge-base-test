### Dispatcher Role in Hub Worker Loading — Route Plan Communication Gap
- **Date:** 01 Jun 2026
- **Author:** Prodapp UX team
- **Source:** PDF — Hub Worker Loading UX Research Synthesis
- **Tags:** #dispatcher #route-plan #communication #glow #hubworker

Dispatchers create route plans in Glow but hub workers are not yet involved at this planning stage. The route plan is not always visible to the HW in the Hubworker app, and dispatcher changes overnight are not always propagated in time. HWs learn their tasks via dispatcher verbal briefing or loading map — no single system connects dispatcher plan to HW task view.

### Licence Plate Lookup — Physical Not Digital
- **Date:** 01 Jun 2026
- **Author:** Prodapp UX team
- **Source:** PDF — Hub Worker Loading UX Research Synthesis
- **Tags:** #dispatcher #licence-plate #lookup #hubworker #mvp

The licence plate for each truck can only be found inside the truck cab. HWs must physically enter the truck or rely on memory to enter the correct truck identifier. No integration with a digital licence-plate lookup exists in Hubworker. MVP design opportunity: allow dispatchers to pre-enter licence plates or provide a lookup so HWs never need to physically enter the cab to find this.

### Confirmation Role Blur — HW vs Driver vs Dispatcher
- **Date:** 01 Jun 2026
- **Author:** Prodapp UX team
- **Source:** PDF — Hub Worker Loading UX Research Synthesis
- **Tags:** #dispatcher #confirmation #role-blur #driver #hubworker #open-question

Ownership of the load confirmation step is unclear and varies by terminal type. Both the Driver App and Hubworker claim the non-scannable confirmation step. Sometimes the driver completes what the HW started. This role overlap requires stakeholder alignment before design — the confirmation ownership model must be explicitly defined per terminal context.
