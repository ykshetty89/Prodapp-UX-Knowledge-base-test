### Glow Status Events — Event-Driven Load Tracking
- **Date:** 01 Jun 2026
- **Author:** Prodapp UX team
- **Source:** PDF — Hub Worker Loading UX Research Synthesis
- **Tags:** #tracking #glow #status-events #confirmation #partial-updates

All status updates — non-scannable goods entry, load confirmation — must trigger events to Glow. The system is event-driven: partial updates are possible (multiple saves) but the final confirmed state is what Glow and the driver rely on for route and tracking accuracy. Any UX flow must ensure Glow receives complete and accurate events at each stage, especially for non-scannable items which are currently often omitted.

### Pre-loading State Visibility — Undefined Confirmed State
- **Date:** 01 Jun 2026
- **Author:** Prodapp UX team
- **Source:** PDF — Hub Worker Loading UX Research Synthesis
- **Tags:** #tracking #preloading #state #glow #time-decoupling #open-question

When pre-loading happens days before the driver arrives, 'confirmed' has an undefined meaning in the current system. There is no distinction between 'ready to load', 'loaded', and 'driver confirmed' states in Glow or Hubworker. This creates tracking ambiguity for dispatchers and operations teams monitoring terminal status in real time.
