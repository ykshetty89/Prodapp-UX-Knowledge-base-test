### Delivery Confirmation Scope — Per Stop vs Per Route (Open Question)
- **Date:** 01 Jun 2026
- **Author:** Prodapp UX team
- **Source:** PDF — Hub Worker Loading UX Research Synthesis
- **Tags:** #delivery #confirmation #per-stop #per-route #open-question #ux-decision

Whether confirmation happens per delivery stop or per entire route is explicitly unresolved in the research and needs further discussion. UX must explore both patterns before committing. The current Confirm button in Hubworker triggers a Glow status event but the scope is unclear — HW marks complete while items may still be missing, and the driver can also modify after HW confirms.

### Non-Scannable Goods — Driver Discovers Discrepancy at Customer
- **Date:** 01 Jun 2026
- **Author:** Prodapp UX team
- **Source:** PDF — Hub Worker Loading UX Research Synthesis
- **Tags:** #delivery #non-scannable #discrepancy #customer #driver #glow

If non-scannable items are never entered by the HW, Glow is never updated, and the driver discovers the discrepancy at the customer location. This creates a driver-customer dispute downstream. The root cause is the absence of proactive non-scannable item prompting in Hubworker at the loading stage — a display gap, not a data gap.
